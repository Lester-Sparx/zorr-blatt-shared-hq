`cheekbones → narrow jaw → neat chin`

Do not merely strengthen outline.

- bridge width;
- side-wall placement;
- broader alar base;

`BROAD NOSE ≠ THICK OUTLINE`

- planar volume;
- light/shadow mass;

### 5. CHIN

- somewhat broader;
- shorter in vertical read;
- less perfectly centered/symmetrical;
- not pointed;
- not superhero-blocked.

- subtle mouth-corner difference;

- scars;
- markings;
- facial damage;
- exaggerated crookedness.
