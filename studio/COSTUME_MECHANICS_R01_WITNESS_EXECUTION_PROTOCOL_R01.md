`POSE DRIVER -> BODY FORCE -> INVARIANT ATTACHMENTS -> FORCE-LOCALIZED FOLD FIELD -> CALM REGION -> FAILURE CHECK`

## REQUIRED WITNESS SET

Exactly six gate witnesses are required. No decorative alternates are part of this gate.

### W01 — ARM_RAISE

Primary proof:

`humerus elevation -> shoulder seam displacement -> armpit force -> upper side-chest response`

Must visibly preserve:

- shoulder seam seated on shoulder block;
- sleeve-cap / armhole attachment;
- collar-lapel root attachment;
- button/front plane independence from the raised arm.

Required fold evidence:

- armpit closing-side compression;
- directional sleeve/armhole tension;
- unaffected lower/opposite jacket planes remain calm.

Immediate FAIL if:

`shoulder seam floats / lapel root detaches / entire jacket wrinkles / sleeve becomes rubber-balloon geometry`.

### W02 — ELBOW_90

Primary proof:

`elbow flexion toward ~90 degrees -> inner sleeve compression -> outer sleeve tension -> distal cuff/watch rotation`

Must visibly preserve:

- sleeve continuity from upper arm to cuff;
- cuff tied to local forearm/wrist orientation;
- watch on the accepted wrist side;
- canonical hand scale/model.

Required fold evidence:

- localized inner-elbow compression;
- quiet outer sleeve with limited tension shapes;
- no accordion noise.

Immediate FAIL if:

`watch drift / cuff drift / forearm length change / hand redesign / sleeve accordion noise`.

### W03 — TORSO_TWIST

Primary proof:

`ribcage yaw relative to pelvis -> one front plane shortens -> opposite plane stretches -> lapel/waist shear resolves coherently`

Must visibly preserve:

- collar/lapel root on chest/neck;
- jacket waist relation to pelvis/waist;
- button spacing logic on garment plane;
- belt attached to pelvis/waist.

Required fold evidence:

- limited diagonal tension on stretched side;
- limited compression on shortened waist side;
- broad lapel planes survive.

Immediate FAIL if:

`lapel shears off chest / buttons become screen-fixed / jacket rotates as rubber tube / waist anchor drifts`.

### W04 — SEATED_COMPRESSION

Primary proof:

`hip flexion + support/contact -> front waist closes -> jacket hem displaces -> trouser waist/crotch compresses`

Must visibly preserve:

- jacket waist attachment;
- belt/trouser waist attachment to pelvis;
- original jacket length family despite temporary hem lift;
- body-authority crotch construction.

Required fold evidence:

- localized waist compression;
- bounded jacket-hem displacement;
- only force-required trouser/crotch compression;
- upper chest/lapels remain calm.

Immediate FAIL if:

`jacket becomes a shorter design / belt floats / crotch is redesigned / realistic micro-wrinkle field appears`.

### W05 — KNEE_BEND

Primary proof:

`knee flexion -> closing-side trouser compression -> opposite/front tension over knee volume`

Must visibly preserve:

- one large trouser-tube read from hip to ankle;
- knee located by body construction;
- trouser cut/seam family;
- ankle/shoe relation.

Required fold evidence:

- localized closing-side compression;
- limited opposing tension shapes;
- mid-thigh and lower-shin calm planes.

Immediate FAIL if:

`accordion leg / knee migration / trouser cut change / anatomy volume disappears under folds`.

### W06 — STRIDE_ANKLE

Primary proof:

`hip separation + leg-chain direction -> trouser tube follows hip/knee/ankle -> hem resolves at ankle/shoe contact`

Must visibly preserve:

- trouser waist on pelvis;
- knee on body chain;
- hem related to ankle/shoe rather than screen position;
- accepted shoe family.

Required fold evidence:

- directional motion/tension folds only where required;
- bounded trouser break at planted ankle/shoe contact;
- cleaner swing leg where compression force is absent.

Immediate FAIL if:

`shoe disconnect / floating ankle break / equal wrinkle noise on both legs / hidden shoe redesign`.
