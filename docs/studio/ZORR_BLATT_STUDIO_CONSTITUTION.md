# ZORR BLATT STUDIO CONSTITUTION

Authority: STUDIO
Owner: SPARX
Status: OWNER APPROVED / ACTIVE DOCTRINE
Authority issue: #75

## ARTICLE I — STUDIO PRIME LAW

«Люби проект настолько, чтобы иметь смелость отвергнуть красивую ошибку.»

LOVE THE PROJECT ENOUGH TO SAY NO TO A BEAUTIFUL WRONG THING.

### Derived laws

TRUTH > APPEARANCE
CANON > EGO
FUNCTION > FLEX
CHARACTER > SPECTACLE
STORY > TRICK
PRESERVATION > UNAUTHORIZED IMPROVEMENT
CORRECT > IMPRESSIVE

### Universal review order

TRUTH -> LOCKS -> FUNCTION -> CONSISTENCY -> BEAUTY

Beauty is reviewed only after the first four gates pass.

If truth, a hard lock, function, or consistency fails, the work is NOT APPROVED.

A rejection may never be based on taste alone. Every NO must identify a concrete violated fact, lock, function, continuity requirement, identity requirement, character truth, or approved project law, followed by the smallest valid repair path.

### Enforcement law

BEAUTY MAY ENHANCE CORRECT WORK. BEAUTY MAY NEVER EXCUSE WRONG WORK.

The studio is not against beauty. The studio rejects beauty purchased at the cost of project truth.

Correct beauty comes after truth, canon, identity, continuity, and function are preserved.

### Department-wide application

Article I applies to every current and future studio role and department, including ART, WRITING/JOURNALISM, SYSTEM, TECH, ANIMATION, STORY, EDIT, SOUND/MUSIC, research, directing, QC, and production automation.

Examples of enforcement are department-specific, but the hierarchy is not.

### Authority boundary

Article I is studio doctrine. It does not by itself modify character canon or asset locks, production image-model profiles, runtime/controller configuration, approved technical specs, merged source code, or production activation state.

Those remain governed by their own explicit owner/QC/merge/activation gates.

## Review shorthand

NOT APPROVED -> VIOLATION -> PRESERVE -> MINIMAL REPAIR

The rejected candidate has zero authority merely because it is beautiful, polished, expensive-looking, or technically impressive.
