## Source-derived mechanisms

The OWNER-provided study groups serious anime graphic-emotion techniques into three useful families.

### 1. Shadow / "Kage" emphasis

Working source terminology: "Kage" is used here as a shorthand for shadow-driven emotional emphasis.

Observed mechanisms from the supplied notes:

- Upper-face shadow / eyes hidden in dense shadow can emphasize depression, concealed rage, or internal collapse.
- Hard shadow masks dividing the face can communicate duality or the moment of a frightening decision.

ZORR extraction:

`EMOTION -> VALUE REORGANIZATION`

The shadow is not decoration. It changes which facial information survives.

Preferred operations:

- EYE-ZONE BLACK MASS
- HALF-FACE SHADOW MASK
- SHADOW PRESSURE FROM ABOVE
- ASYMMETRIC SHADOW SPLIT
- LIGHT REMOVAL RATHER THAN GLOW ADDITION

Adult-fashion rule: preserve clean hair, silhouette, collar, jewelry, and garment hierarchy while allowing the face to enter a harsher value state.

### 2. Anatomical realism + microexpression

Source-derived mechanisms:

- Wrinkles and veins may appear in moments of rage or despair.
- Neck-muscle tension, glabella folds, eye bags, clenched teeth and gum detail can reduce cartoonish softness.
- Microexpression can replace broad caricature: one tear, lower-lip tremor, eyelid tic, tiny jaw tension.

ZORR extraction:

`ADULT EMOTION = MACRO CONTROL + MICRO FAILURE`

The strongest serious expression does not require the whole face to deform equally.

Preferred focus zones:

- glabella / brow compression
- lower eyelid tension
- orbicularis / eye-socket pressure
- nasolabial tension only when justified
- masseter / jaw compression
- neck tendon / sternocleidomastoid tension
- lip-corner asymmetry
- lower-lip tremor
- one tear or one sweat trace only when narratively useful

Anti-rule: do not add every anatomical stress marker at once. Choose 1-3 dominant signals.

### 3. Drawing-mode and frame distortion

Source-derived mechanisms:

- During madness or panic, clean line art may be replaced by rough, broken, chaotic pencil-like marks.
- A Dutch angle can destabilize face and space to create bodily unease in the viewer.

ZORR extraction:

`EMOTIONAL BREAK -> DRAWING SYSTEM BREAK`

Possible operators:

- CLEAN LINE -> ROUGH HATCH
- SMOOTH CEL -> DRY GRAPHITE / SCRATCH STATE
- STABLE CONTOUR -> DISCONTINUOUS CONTOUR
- NORMAL PERSPECTIVE -> LOCAL DUTCH / MISALIGNMENT
- CLEAN FACE -> ONE REGION WITH DIFFERENT RENDER RULES

The whole image should not become noisy by default. A local drawing-mode switch is usually stronger than global chaos.

## ZORR hierarchy

For adult psykodelic fashion emotion:

1. ACTING FIRST
2. VALUE / SHADOW SECOND
3. MICROANATOMY THIRD
4. DRAWING-MODE BREAK FOURTH
5. CAMERA DISTORTION FIFTH
6. SUPPORT FX LAST

A good shot can stop after step 2 or 3. More layers are not automatically better.

## Practical emotional recipes

### CONTROLLED RAGE

- closed mouth
- compressed brows / glabella
- visible jaw and neck tension
- upper-face shadow pressure
- one rough-hatch region around one eye or cheek
- no glowing eyes
- no particle aura required

### DESPAIR / INTERNAL COLLAPSE

- heavy upper-face shadow
- reduced eye catchlight
- one tear or lower-lip tremor
- line quality becomes slightly unstable around the face only
- background remains calm enough to make the face failure visible

### PANIC / MENTAL BREAK

- widened or asymmetrical eyes
- breathing / mouth tension
- local render-state fracture
- selective rough lines replacing clean line art
- dutch angle only if it strengthens the bodily unease

### FRIGHTENING DECISION

- face nearly still
- hard shadow mask dividing the face
- minimal microexpression
- one tiny eye or jaw change
- restrained camera

## Integration with existing ZORR language

This study extends, rather than replaces:

- GRAPHIC EMOTION
- BLACK MASS
- LINE SUBTRACTION
- RENDER-STATE FRACTURE
- LOCAL COLOR MODE SWITCH
- SOFT SPATIAL PRESSURE

New emphasis:

`PSYKEDELIC DOES NOT REQUIRE COLOR OR PATTERN.`

A face can become psychedelic through incompatible shadow logic, anatomy stress, drawing-mode fracture, or spatial/camera instability alone.

## Anti-patterns

- glowing eyes as default rage symbol
- full-frame rough hatching for every intense emotion
- random veins / wrinkles added without anatomical logic
- Dutch angle used merely because the scene is "dark"
- hard shadow masks that do not improve emotional read
- FX overwhelming the fashion silhouette
- multiple simultaneous distortion tricks with no hierarchy
