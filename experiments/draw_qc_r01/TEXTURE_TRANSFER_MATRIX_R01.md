## Durable authority from #199

Global texture law:

`TEXTURE != NOISE`

Every material:

`BASE FIELD + MACRO FORM + STRUCTURAL EDGES + LIMITED MESO VARIATION + SPARSE MICRO ACCENTS + LIGHT`

Frequency law:

`MACRO > MESO > MICRO`

Default ordinary production surface envelope at intended screen scale:

- calm area usually `>=85%`
- structural/detail edge coverage usually `<=10%`
- main material palette roughly `4–6` value/color bands
- coarse-to-fine energy `E16/E1 >=2` preferred for calm materials
- fine residual p90 in calm zones `<=2% luminance` preferred
- random uniform grain = FAIL
- photo texture pasted over simplified geometry = FAIL
- structure-unrelated cracks/scratches/grain = FAIL
- distance-invariant microdetail = FAIL

Distance law:

- NEAR = macro + meso + sparse selected micro
- MID = macro + meso; micro collapses
- FAR = silhouette + large value/color masses

Important boundary: material-specific identity may locally exceed the ordinary envelope, but may not become uncontrolled high-frequency noise.

## Current transfer hypothesis

The profitable ZORR texture direction is not globally flat and not globally gritty.

`CURRENT WINNER CANDIDATE = STRUCTURED-SPARSE / SELECTIVE MATERIAL INFORMATION`

Core production hypothesis:

`CALM LARGE MASS -> STRUCTURAL FORM -> MATERIAL-SPECIFIC MESO -> SPARSE FOCAL MICRO -> STOP`

High-detail islands require calmer surrounding fields. Texture density is treated as material-, distance-, form-, and importance-dependent rather than as one global image property.

## Cross-material laws extracted from TEX-TEACHER-01

1. `DETAIL != EVERYWHERE`.
2. High-frequency detail is profitable only when surrounded by calmer areas.
3. Material identity should come from different combinations of macro form, edge structure and specular logic rather than from one universal grain overlay.
4. Black materials should usually remain large readable masses; internal detail is subordinate.
5. Metal must read as form/panel/edge/specular before damage.
6. Leather must read as deep field + broad specular shape before any small surface cue.
7. Organic texture must follow anatomy/flow/volume rather than operate as random decoration.
8. Exact gothic motifs/design DNA from TEX-TEACHER-01 are not transferable ZORR laws.

## Frequency budget by shot scale

### NEAR

Allowed:

- macro form
- meso construction/material information
- sparse selected micro accents

Required stop rule:

- micro detail may not obscure primary contour, face planes, black-mass read, or material separation.

### MID

Allowed:

- macro form
- essential meso structure

Collapsed:

- most micro detail
- minor scratches
- generic grain
- tiny ornament internals

### FAR

Allowed:

- silhouette
- large value/color masses
- only identity-critical accents that survive physically at the intended scale

Forbidden by default:

- microtexture
- fine ornament internals
- small scratches
- pores
- tiny fabric information
