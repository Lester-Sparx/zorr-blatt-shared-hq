# ZORR DIVERGENT CONCEPT GATE R02 — FINAL PASS

## Why R01 was rejected
R01 compared free-form strings. That meant lexical renaming could fake novelty: `close three-quarter` and `three-quarter close` could be treated as different even when structurally identical.

R02 replaces raw-string inequality with canonical structural tags and weighted Jaccard distance across 12 dimensions:

- framing
- camera
- pose
- hand role
- face mechanism
- body effort
- space operator
- lighting
- palette
- render language
- environment logic
- temporal state

Hard constraints:
- weighted structural distance >= 0.60
- at least 7 structurally changed dimensions
- key-signature similarity <= 0.48
- repeated key tags enter a 4-concept cooldown
- at least 4 active emotion channels
- far analogy must come from a non-anime functional domain

## Seed failure pattern
The recent repeated pattern was canonically encoded as:

`chest-up portrait + eye-level 3/4 + upright square torso + hand near chest/lapel + brow furrow/direct stare + strong/bound/direct/sustained body + dark void + colored rim/hard contrast + cream/dark accent palette + dense hatching + abstract empty environment + static emotional peak`

This is now treated as a known fixation signature, not as a reusable default.

## First strict R02 run
R01 had passed all 12 concepts.
R02 immediately rejected 7/12 because several proposals still reused structural traits hidden by different wording. This proved the stricter gate was doing real work.

Rejected examples included:
- static camera repeated too often
- eye-level repeated too often
- wide lens repeated too often
- shallow depth repeated too often
- neutral-brow mechanism repeated too often
- low-motion/upright body construction repeated too often

The gate was NOT weakened. The concepts were rebuilt.

## Final 12 accepted concept constructions

1. **Relief after controlled panic** — extreme-wide tiny figure, overhead slow crane, seated spine uncoiling, palms releasing support, jaw release / wet lower lid, large empty halo, high-key diffuse aftermath.
2. **Tenderness inhibited by fear** — profile relational medium, high-oblique slow dolly, body leans then stops, hovering hand before touch, held breath / inner-brow lift, compressed interpersonal gap.
3. **Suppressed public humiliation** — crowd tableau, high balcony telephoto, controlled walking stride rather than static portrait, hidden grip in clothing, chin up / eyes down / lip compression, institutional flat light.
4. **Euphoric release** — full-body low canted tracking shot, spine arch and open arms mid-turn, splayed hands, open laugh, radial spatial expansion, white-gold backlight burst.
5. **Quiet predatory curiosity** — reflection-split medium-wide, floor-level lateral slide, crouched circling weight shift, fingertip tracing condensation, neutral brow / lower-lid tension / nostril change, false reflection logic.
6. **Numb grief** — back view with mirror multiplicity, high-oblique slow lateral dolly, weight sunk with repetitive sway, loose hands under water, unfocused reflected face, washed-ink edge dissolution.
7. **Mischievous joy** — asymmetric waist-wide shot, low side handheld normal lens, hips already leaving while torso twists, coin misdirection behind back, cheek lift and escaping glance, diagonal escape path.
8. **Jealousy under manners** — compressed social tableau, telephoto seated eyeline, torso turns away while head remains socially engaged, thumbnail micro-scrapes cup rim, polite smile + sideways eyes, warm room / cold glass split.
9. **Shame compressing into rage** — hands dominate foreground from table height, hunched core coils upward, both hands crush unseen edge, emotion carried by masseter/throat rather than eyes, vertical ceiling pressure with forced deep axis.
10. **Awe** — monumental extreme-wide from behind, worm-eye telephoto with slow tilt up, small figure leans into overwhelming scale, hand shields light without defense, minimal face, suspended impossible textile planes.
11. **Moral disgust** — strict profile close, orthographic side view, waist and shoulders recoil, hand pinches own sleeve to avoid contact, nose wrinkle / upper-lip raise / jaw retract, high-key clinical side light.
12. **Calm dominance** — distant full-body back view with small mirror face, centered long-lens slow dolly out, relaxed crossed ankles, open hand on armrest, half-lidded neutral face, empty chairs orient toward subject like a gravity field.

Every accepted concept activates 6–7 independent emotion channels and passes the strict canonical-tag gate against both the original fixation history and previously accepted concepts.

## New ZORR law
Different emotion labels are irrelevant unless the underlying construction changes.

A concept is not new because color, expression label, or FX changed. It is new only when enough of the following change structurally:

`camera + framing + body behavior + hand function + face mechanism + space + light + environment + temporal state`

Image generation remains downstream of this gate, never the ideation mechanism itself.
