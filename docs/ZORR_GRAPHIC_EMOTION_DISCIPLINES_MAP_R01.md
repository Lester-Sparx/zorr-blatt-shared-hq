# ZORR GRAPHIC EMOTION — DISCIPLINES MAP R01

Status: PROVISIONAL PRODUCTION STUDY
Lore status: NON-LORE

## Reference observation

The studied close-up uses an extreme crop centered on brow/eyes, dense form-following hatching, cross-hatching, large black hair masses, very bright eye whites, compressed value range in skin, and vertical emotional/effect lines in the background. The emotional read is rage / pressure / fixation rather than a decorative FX state.

## Key terminology

- **Hatching** — parallel or near-parallel lines used to build tone and form.
- **Cross-hatching** — intersecting hatching layers used to deepen shadow and texture.
- **Contour / form hatching** — hatch direction follows the facial volume rather than sitting flat on the page.
- **Kakeami (カケアミ)** — common manga hatching pattern / bundled hatch language used for tone and atmosphere.
- **Beta (ベタ)** — solid black fill; useful as a designed black mass.
- **Kouka-sen (効果線)** — effect lines; graphic lines used for motion, force, atmosphere, or emotion.
- **Tate-sen (縦線)** — vertical effect lines often used for negative emotional pressure, gloom, anxiety, shock, or tension depending on context.
- **Value grouping / black-mass design** — organizing the image into large light/dark shapes before micro-detail.
- **Microexpression design** — brow compression, eyelid tension, glabella folds, jaw set, lip tension, nostril flare, neck tension.
- **Extreme close-up (ECU)** — framing that removes most contextual information and forces attention onto a tiny emotional region.

## Why the reference works

The image does not rely on glow, aura, or environmental spectacle. Pressure comes from the hierarchy:

`EYES -> BROW / GLABELLA -> HATCHED FACE PLANES -> BLACK HAIR MASS -> VERTICAL EFFECT LINES`

The highest local contrast is at the eyes. Hatching density increases around brow, eye sockets, nose bridge, and cheeks, so line density becomes emotional pressure. The hair acts as a near-solid black cap that compresses the face from above. The crop removes escape space.

## ZORR extraction

For adult fashion anime, preserve elegance in costume and silhouette while allowing the face to temporarily enter a different drawing state.

Useful operators:

- `HATCH_DENSITY_PRESSURE`
- `FORM_HATCH_WRAP`
- `BLACK_CAP_COMPRESSION`
- `EYE_CONTRAST_ANCHOR`
- `GLABELLA_STRESS`
- `ECU_CROP_PRESSURE`
- `LOCAL_RENDER_STATE_FRACTURE`
- `VERTICAL_EFFECT_PRESSURE`
- `LINE_SUBTRACTION_OUTSIDE_FOCUS`

Rule:

`GRAPHIC EMOTION = ACTING + VALUE HIERARCHY + LINE-DENSITY MODULATION + FRAMING`

Do not reduce this to a permanent hatch texture pasted over every face. Hatching is an emotional-state operator and should be localized by anatomy, light, and dramatic intent.

## Training sequence

1. Same face, neutral clean line.
2. Add brow / glabella microexpression only.
3. Add form hatching around eye sockets and nose bridge.
4. Increase hatching density only in the emotional center.
5. Collapse hair / upper face into larger black mass.
6. Test ECU crop.
7. Add effect-line field only if it strengthens the emotion.
8. Remove all nonessential FX and compare.

PASS requires the emotion to read even if the background is removed.
